05 — Asked daily at the counter

05

Top 5 Questions Answered

Clear answers on GST compliance, authentic OEM stock, same-day van dispatches, and credit terms for RIICO plant heads.

01

Do I get a 100% compliant GST Tax Invoice with HSN codes?

Yes, absolutely. Every single purchase carries an itemized GST tax invoice with mandatory 8-digit HSN codes (e.g., 8482 for bearings, 4010 for transmission belts). Share your plant’s GSTIN once—it maps directly into our system for seamless monthly GSTR-1 & 2B input credit reconciliation.

GSTR-1 Invoiced HSN 8482 / 4010 100% Tax Credit
02

Are all bearings, belts, and valves 100% genuine boxed OEM stock?

Strict zero-counterfeit policy. We only source direct factory-sealed stock from SKF, FAG Schaeffler, NBC, Polymatic, Mitsuboshi, Gates, and Unbrako. Every bearing and valve comes in its original manufacturer carton with intact holographic QR tamper seals and manufacturer test certificates on request.

Authorized Polymatic Hub Sealed Factory Boxes Test Certs on Request
03

How fast is same-day delivery across the RIICO Neemrana industrial belt?

For line breakdowns and urgent maintenance parts, our dedicated delivery vans dispatch within 25–40 minutes across RIICO Neemrana Phase I, II, Japanese Zone, Ghiloth, Behror, and Keshwana. For planned project BOQs across India, orders stage and ship via express cargo.

25–40 Min Plant Dispatch Japanese Zone & Ghiloth Breakdown Priority
04

Can our factory set up monthly credit terms against formal Purchase Orders?

Yes. Registered manufacturing units and regular industrial clients can transition to 30-day billing terms against official plant POs following three initial transactions. Consolidated monthly statements are dispatched on the 1st of each month with comprehensive delivery challans attached.

30-Day Plant Credit PO Billing Supported Monthly Statements
05

Can you source rare, obsolete, or specialized non-catalog machinery spares?

Yes. If you need custom bore sizes, high-temperature Viton seals, specialized timing belt pitch lengths, or discontinued pneumatic cylinders, send us a photo of the nameplate or sample dimensions. We quote exact lead times and OEM equivalents within 2 hours.

Rare Spares Sourcing High-Temp Viton & PU 2-Hour Lead Time Quote
