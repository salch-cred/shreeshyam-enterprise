Asked daily at the counter

Will I get a GST invoice with HSN codes?

Yes, on every sale. Share your GSTIN once and it goes on each bill, with the HSN code printed against each line.

Is the stock genuine?

We deal in authorised makes in their original packing. For pneumatics we are the authorised Polymatic distributor. Ask for a test certificate if your plant needs one.

Where do you deliver the same day?

Across the RIICO Neemrana belt, including Phase I and II, the Japanese Zone, Ghiloth, Behror and Keshwana. Project orders go out across India by transport.

Can we buy on credit?

Plants can move to 30-day credit against a PO after three cash bills. You get a statement on the 1st and a due date on every bill.

Can you find a part that isn't in the catalogue?

Usually. Send a nameplate photo or sample dimensions for a rare size, an obsolete cylinder or a special belt length, and we will come back with the lead time or an equivalent.
